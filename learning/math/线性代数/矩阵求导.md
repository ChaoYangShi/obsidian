> 对未知参数向量 ${}x{}$ 中的每一个分量分别求偏导数,然后将所有偏导数按顺序排列成一个列向量,得到梯度向量 ${}\nabla J(x){}$ 

$$
\nabla _{x}J(x) = \begin{bmatrix}
\frac{\partial J}{\partial x_{1}} \\
\frac{\partial J}{\partial x_{2}} \\
\vdots \\
\frac{\partial J}{\partial x_{n}} \\
\end{bmatrix}
$$
### 1. 分量求偏导法
设观测方程第 ${}i{}$ 行对应的残差为 ${}r_{i}{}$
$$
r_{i} = (Ax-b)_{i} = \sum\limits^{n}_{j=1}A_{ij}x_{i}-b_{i}
$$
目标函数 ${}J(x){}$ 写成标量形式 
$$
J(x) = \frac{1}{2}\sum \limits_{i=1}^{m}r_{i}^2 = \frac{1}{2}\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}\left( \sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}A_{ij}x_{i}-b_{i} \right)^2
$$
对某一个未知数 ${}x_{k}(k \in {1,2,\dots,n}){}$ 求偏导 
$$
\frac{\partial J}{\partial x_{k}} = \sum \limits_{i=1}^m r_{i} \frac{\partial r_{i}}{\partial x_{k}}
$$
由于变量之间相互独立,仅当 ${}j = k{}$ 时导数不为零
$$
\frac{\partial r_{i}}{\partial x_{k}} = \frac{\partial }{\partial x_{k}}(\sum\limits^{n}_{j=1}A_{ij}x_{i}-b_{i}) = A_{ik}
$$
带入偏导数表达式 
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial J}{\partial x_{k}} &= \sum \limits_{i=1}^mr_{i}A_{ik}=\sum \limits_{i=1}^m(A^T)_{ki} r_{i} \\
\nabla _{x}J(x) &=  \begin{bmatrix}
\frac{\partial J}{\partial x_{1}} \\
\frac{\partial J}{\partial x_{2}} \\
\vdots \\
\frac{\partial J}{\partial x_{n}} \\
\end{bmatrix} = A^Tr = A^T(Ax-b) = A^TAx - A^Tb
\end{align}
$$
### 2. 矩阵向量求导法则
对展开后的代数式进行求导
$$
J(x) = \frac{1}{2}x^TA^TAx - b^TAx + \frac{1}{2}b^Tb
$$
矩阵微分中常用的恒等式
1. **二次型导数** : 若 ${}M{}$ 为对称矩阵,则 ${}\nabla_{x}(x^TMx)=2Mx{}$
	- 这里 ${}M=A^TA{}$ 显然为对称矩阵,所以
	$$
	  \nabla_{x}\left( \frac{1}{2}x^TA^TAx \right) = A^TAx
	  $$
2. **线性项导数** : 对于任意常量 ${}c{}$ ,有 ${}\nabla_{x}(c^Tx) = c{}$
	- ${}b^TAx = (A^Tb)^Tx{}$ 
	- 令 ${}c = A^Tb{}$ ,则
		$$
		\nabla_{x}(b^TAx) = \nabla_{x}(A^Tb)^Tx = A^Tb
		$$
3. **常数项导数** : 导数为零向量
合并三项得: 
$$
\nabla_{x}J(x) = A^TAx - A^Tb
$$
### 3. 链式法则
引入中间变量 -- 残差向量 ${}r(x) = Ax-b \in R^m{}$
- 外层函数: ${}f(x) = \frac{1}{2}||r||^2_{2} = \frac{1}{2}r^Tr{}$
- 内层映射: ${}r(x) = Ax - b{}$
求两层的微分
- ${}\nabla_{r}f = r{}$
- ${}\frac{\partial r}{\partial x} = A{}$
应用多变量链式法则: 
$$
\nabla_{x}J(x) = \left( \frac{\partial r}{\partial x} \right)^T \nabla_{r}f = A^Tr = A^T(Ax-b) = A^TAx - A^Tb
$$
